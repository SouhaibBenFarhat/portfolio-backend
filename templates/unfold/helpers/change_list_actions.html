Unfold renders the bulk-action bar as a FIXED element pinned to the bottom of the viewport, which put it on top of the sticky save bar — two bars in the same place, the Save button half-visible behind the other. Hiding one while the other showed was the first attempt and it was wrong too: they are not alternatives, they are both "things you can do to this list". So the wrapper is now plain: no fixed positioning, no viewport pinning, no left offset tracking the sidebar. It renders wherever it is placed — which is inside the save bar in admin/change_list.html, alongside Save. One bar, both controls, nothing overlapping.
