How it works

Three steps, then it answers for you.

  1. 1

    Feed it your CV and sources

    Upload your CV, then connect GitHub, LinkedIn and any documents. It reads all of it — so every answer comes from your own material, never something it made up.

  2. 2

    Add the facts a CV leaves out

    Salary expectations, location, notice period, how you like to work — the questions recruiters always ask and a CV never answers. You write them once.

  3. 3

    Share your page — it takes the questions

    You get you.hirees.me. Recruiters ask; it answers from everything above, in your own words, day or night — no PDF, no waiting on you to reply.
