How it works

One file in. The rest is ours.

01
Start with what you already have
Your CV, once. We read it, pull out the roles, skills and dates, and draft the page from them — so you begin by correcting something, never by facing a blank form.
02
Get an address with your name on it
A quiet, fast page at you.hirees.me — the kind of link worth putting at the top of an application instead of an attachment nobody opens.
03
Then it takes the questions
Notice period, visa status, whether you've really used Kafka — the things that actually decide it, answered at the moment they're asked rather than a week later.

The rules it follows

It only ever says what you've said.

It doesn't invent
Every answer comes from your CV and the facts you've written. Asked something you haven't covered, it says so instead of guessing.
You keep the last word
Read every conversation it has had. Correct a wrong answer once and it stays corrected — and you can take the whole page down whenever you like.
European by default
Your CV is processed in the European Union by a European model provider, not shipped across the Atlantic to train something else.
