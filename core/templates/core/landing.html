How it works

Three steps, then it answers for you.

1
Feed it your CV and sources
Upload your CV, add your GitHub username, and drop in anything else — a cover letter, a certificate, a reference. It reads all of it — so every answer comes from your own material, never something it made up.
2
Add the facts a CV leaves out
Salary expectations, location, notice period, how you like to work — the questions recruiters always ask and a CV never answers. You write them once.
3
Share your page — it takes the questions
You get you.hirees.me. Recruiters ask; it answers from everything above, in your own words, day or night — no PDF, no waiting on you to reply. They just open the link — no account, nothing to install.

The rules it follows

It only ever says what you've said.

It doesn't invent
Every answer comes from your CV and the facts you've written. Asked something you haven't covered, it says so instead of guessing.
You keep the last word
Read every conversation it has had. Correct a wrong answer once and it stays corrected — and you can take the whole page down whenever you like.
European by default
Your CV is processed in the European Union by a European model provider, not shipped across the Atlantic to train something else.
